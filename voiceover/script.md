# Demo narration script (< 3 min, ~330 words @ ~150wpm)
Voice: Polly Matthew (neural, en-US) — deliberately NOT Danielle, so the
narrator never sounds like Alexa. MP3s in `mp3/`, generated by
`../scripts/gen-narration.mjs`. Step 5: mix under Playwright footage.

## seg1-title (0:00–0:15)
Third place with Coffee Meets Codex. Now — what if you could just say it?
Music Meets Alexa. Meet the person behind the playlist.

## seg2-terminal (0:15–0:45)
Under the hood, a self-hosted MCP server speaking spec 2025-11-25 over
Streamable HTTP. Six music-connection tools, automated checks, all green.
It also powers a simulated Alexa-plus experience in the browser —
no Echo required.

## seg3-hero (0:45–1:20)
Here is the front page: some people just get your playlist. Three fictional
music lovers, each with a record-label badge — genre, tempo, catalog number —
and one line that could start a conversation. Teasers out loud, full stories
on your private screen. Because Alexa lives in the living room.

## seg4-profile (1:20–1:50)
Before saving anything, set your corner. Your name, eighteen plus, friend or
lover or both, your moods. It stays in this browser only — the server gets a
yes-flag, never your name.

## seg5-why-music (1:50–2:25)
Why Jules? Two grounded reasons, one honest unknown — never a score. And every
person has their own original loop. Tap Mika's label, and her city-pop actually
plays, with a heartbeat line dancing along. All composed in code, no
copyrighted audio.

## seg6-save (2:25–2:45)
One yes stays pending. A second yes makes it real. Nobody is ever contacted
from this demo — and every call you just saw is real JSON-RPC. Watch the trace.

## seg7-closing (2:45–3:00)
Music Meets Alexa — built in twelve days on top of our third-place hack, for
the Alexa-plus track. A little curiosity, a real possibility. Now you say it.
