# Demo narration script (~2:58, 8 segments — storyboard by 大G)
Voice: Polly Matthew (neural, en-US) — deliberately NOT Danielle, so the
narrator never sounds like Alexa. MP3s in `mp3/`, generated by
`../scripts/gen-narration.mjs`. Step 5: mix under Playwright footage.
Rule: narration and Danielle never overlap — pauses hold site audio.

## seg1-hook (0:00–0:17)
A playlist can tell you a lot about someone. Their mood, their memories, the
way they see the world. What if music could also start a conversation?

## seg2-meet (0:17–0:41)
Meet Music Meets Alexa — a voice-first music discovery experience. Choose
friendship or a deeper connection, then ask for your daily listening brief.
Three fictional people appear, each with a musical personality of their own.

## seg3-hear (0:41–1:08)
Start with a short introduction. Ask to see more, and a full profile opens on
your private screen. Tap a record label to hear an original melody,
synthesized live in the browser. Even the heartbeat follows the real audio.

## seg4-why (1:08–1:30)
Ask why someone was recommended. You will get two concrete reasons, plus
something we honestly do not know. You can even create a three-track mixtape,
with a suggested opening line to start a conversation.

## seg5-privacy (1:30–1:55)
Because voice assistants often live in shared spaces, privacy shapes every
step. Spoken introductions stay brief. Full profiles appear only when
requested. And your own music profile stays in your browser — never on
the server.

## seg6-yeses (1:55–2:18)
If you would like to save an interest, the system asks twice. Only after both
confirmations does it become Pending. Nobody is contacted automatically.
Just a little celebration for a thoughtful choice.

## seg7-tech (2:18–2:45)
Behind this playful interface is a real, self-hosted MCP server with six
tools, using Streamable HTTP and JSON-RPC. Our browser-based Alexa-plus
simulation calls those tools live. We use Amazon Polly's Danielle voice for
consistent narration across browsers — not the official Alexa voice — and
WebAudio to synthesize original music in real time.

## seg8-closing (2:45–2:58)
Music Meets Alexa. A voice-first way to meet someone new. Meet the person behind the playlist.
